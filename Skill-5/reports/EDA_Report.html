The bounty distribution is highly non-uniform, motivating explicit
classification rather than treating raw bounty values as a balanced target.

The 75th percentile creates a data-driven binary target. Feature engineering
extracts structural information from pirate names so that Logistic Regression
can estimate the probability of belonging to the High-Bounty class.

The correlation table is descriptive evidence only. Final judgement of
predictive usefulness is reserved for train/test evaluation and
cross-validation.
