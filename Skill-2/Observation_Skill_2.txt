OBSERVATION - SKILL 2

The One Piece bounty dataset was preprocessed through an 11-step pipeline. The raw file had 165 records and 3 columns, where the bounty was stored as text with currency symbols and commas. After cleaning, 145 characters have a numeric bounty and 20 are marked Unknown, which were flagged instead of being imputed. 1 record(s) with a wrong currency symbol were corrected, 20 placeholder ranks (999) were removed and the rank was rebuilt from the bounty itself. The highest bounty belongs to Gol D. Roger at 5,564,800,000 Berries. The bounty distribution was heavily right-skewed (skewness 4.28). The log10 transformation removed the right skew, but the 2 extremely small bounties (Bepo, Tony Tony Chopper) were flagged as outliers on the log scale and pull the overall skewness to -2.44; without them it is only 0.03, which means the log scale is nearly symmetric. Outliers were flagged, not deleted, because these are real bounties. The cleaned dataset is ready for feature engineering in the next skill.
