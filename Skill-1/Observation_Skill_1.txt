OBSERVATION – SKILL 1

The Titanic dataset was successfully analyzed using Exploratory Data Analysis techniques. The dataset contains 1309 passenger records and 9 main columns including age, fare, sex, family information, passenger class, embarkation point and survival status. Survival was analyzed according to sex, passenger class, age and lifecycle group. The lifecycle analysis showed the highest survival rate for children at 42.55%, while the senior group had the lowest survival rate at 17.50%. The correlation analysis also showed that Sex had a positive correlation of about 0.40 with survival, while Pclass had a negative correlation of about -0.24. The experiment provided useful insights into the factors associated with passenger survival.
